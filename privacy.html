AtomScope Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

What AtomScope Does

AtomScope is a student productivity platform that can connect to Google Classroom to display classroom information such as courses and assignments.

Information We Access

When you connect Google Classroom, AtomScope may access information provided through the Google Classroom API, such as your name, email address, profile picture, courses, and coursework information, depending on the permissions you approve.

How We Use Information

Information accessed through Google Classroom is used to provide AtomScope's classroom and productivity features. We do not sell your personal information.

Google Account Access

You can disconnect your Google Classroom account from AtomScope. You can also manage or revoke third-party access through your Google Account settings.

Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, contact the AtomScope developer through the contact information provided with the app.
